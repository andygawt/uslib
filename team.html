Dimitrios Kouvaras

Junior Researcher

Dimitrios Kouvaras was a visiting researcher at USLIB in 2025. During his time, Dimitrios researched U.S. foreign policy towards Greece in the 1930s and '40s. His project employs Balkanism and modernisation theory as conceptual lenses for understanding how cultural, racial and development perceptions intertwined with geopolitics in influencing American responses to the period's turbulent Greek politics. To this end, Dimitrios received a study grant to consult the archive of the Roosevelt Institute for American Studies. Since his visit, he in the process of composing his findings into a journal article.

Dimitrios is currently pursuing an Advanced MA in European Interdisciplinary Studies at the College of Europe in Natolin as a European History and Civilisation scholar. In 2025, he received a first-class honours degree in History and Archaeology from the University of Athens and also worked on the research project "Antidictatorial Struggle, 1967-1974" at the university's archive. During his undergraduate studies, he completed exchange programmes in Groningen (2024), Madrid (2023) and Rome (2022), gaining interdisciplinary experience in American and Latin American studies, among other fields.
