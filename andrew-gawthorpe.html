Andrew Gawthorpe

Group leader

Andrew Gawthorpe is the principal investigator of USLIB, a university lecturer (from January 1st, 2027 associate professor) at Leiden University, and a Senior Fellow at the Foreign Policy Centre in London. The author of multiple books about U.S. foreign policy and politics, Andrew is one of the most quoted European experts on these topics in international media. He is also the author of the America Explained newsletter.
