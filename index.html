American Foreign Policy and Liberalism

A research project examining the relationship between liberal values and U.S. foreign policy from the twentieth century to the present day.

About the Project

This research group, led by Dr. Andrew Gawthorpe at Leiden University, investigates how liberal and illiberal impulses have shaped American foreign policy. Funded by a €1 million grant from the Dutch Research Council (NWO), the project runs from 2023 to 2027.

Our work examines key questions about the role of liberalism in U.S. foreign policy, including:

  • How have liberal values influenced American foreign policy decisions?
  • When and why has illiberalism played a role in U.S. international relations?
  • What can historical patterns tell us about contemporary American foreign policy?
