About the Project

The "U.S. Foreign Policy and Liberalism" (USLIB) project examines the complex relationship between liberal values and American foreign policy from the twentieth century to the present day. Funded by a €1 million grant from the Dutch Research Council (NWO), this five-year research initiative (2023-2027) investigates how liberal and illiberal impulses have shaped U.S. international relations.

Our research explores key questions about when and how liberal values have influenced American foreign policy decisions, when illiberalism has played a role, and what historical patterns can tell us about contemporary U.S. foreign policy.
