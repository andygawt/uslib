Research Overview

Our research examines the complex relationship between liberal values and American foreign policy from the twentieth century to the present.

Project Description

The "American Foreign Policy and Liberalism" project investigates how liberal and illiberal impulses have shaped U.S. international relations. This five-year research initiative (2023-2027) is funded by a €1 million grant from the Dutch Research Council (NWO).

Research Questions

  • How have liberal values influenced American foreign policy decisions across different historical periods?
  • When and why has the United States pursued illiberal policies abroad?
  • What is the relationship between domestic liberal ideals and foreign policy practices?
  • How do historical patterns of liberalism and illiberalism inform contemporary U.S. foreign policy?

Research Themes

Democracy Promotion and Nation-Building

Examining U.S. efforts to promote democratic governance abroad, from post-WWII reconstruction to contemporary interventions.

Human Rights and Foreign Policy

Analyzing the role of human rights considerations in American foreign policy decision-making.

Illiberalism in U.S. Foreign Policy

Investigating instances where the United States has supported authoritarian regimes or pursued illiberal policies abroad.

Presidential Power and Foreign Policy

Studying how executive power shapes the liberal or illiberal character of U.S. foreign policy.

Methodology

Our research employs a combination of historical analysis, archival research, and contemporary policy analysis. We examine primary sources, government documents, and diplomatic records to understand how liberal values have been interpreted and applied in foreign policy contexts.

Current Projects

Presidential Extra-Territorialization

Examining how U.S. presidents use foreign policy to bypass domestic constraints on executive power.

Civilizational Wilsonianism

Tracing the evolution of Wilsonian ideals in U.S. foreign policy from Woodrow Wilson to the Trump era.

Post-War Nation-Building

Historical analysis of U.S. nation-building efforts and their liberal foundations.
